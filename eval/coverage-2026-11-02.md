## Reading the quality, not the counts

- Verification: 69 live claims are verified — the City of Vancouver inventory (open data, values stored directly) and the survey (the respondent's own words); every literature / database claim is **unverified** by decision D5 (Kay's unified review, checklists in `review/`). Counts of unverified claims are evidence collected, not evidence confirmed.
- Independence: a field with ≥ 2 sources (142 plant × field cells) can show agreement or disagreement; a field with 1 source (62 cells) cannot be cross-checked. 67 disagreements and 1 condition-dependent variations are recorded as data (diagnosis.json), never resolved.
- Locality: 96 live claims are at the Vancouver scale (City of Vancouver trees, iNaturalist Greater Vancouver, survey); the rest are BC, PNW, North America, UK or global and say so in `place`.
- Practitioner knowledge: 32 claims from 9 anonymous respondents; L0 selection reasons are stored but never cited (R2), only L1–L2 notes can be quoted.
- Gaps are listed per plant above (gate fields with no sourced claim) and in `brain/data/kg.json gaps`; they stay empty rather than being filled by the model.
