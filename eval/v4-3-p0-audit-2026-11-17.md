# V4.3 P0 audit — stale areas, Screen on a trimmed outline, accent placement, light words (2026-11-17)

Internal report (not for a public page). Branch `feature/platform-v4-3-visual-intelligence`. No plant record, claim, source,

## 2.1 Stale areas in the Shape inspector — bug, fixed

**Seen.** `brain/screenshots/platform-v4-2/06-shape-l-shape-1440x900.png`: after the outline was cut to an L the inspector header
read "8 × 6 m · 40.32 m² plantable · 3 beds".

**Cause.** `renderShape()` in `design/js/app.js` built that line from `prop.site.plantable_m2`: the figure stored on the layout
proposal when it was computed. An applied outline edit marks the proposal `stale` but does not recompute it (the confirmed beds are
clipped instead), so the header kept the area of the rectangle. "8 × 6 m" was the bounding box of the outline, printed as if it
were the site's size. The layout summary under it ("… m² planted, … m² open") came from the same stored proposal.

**Fix.** The header is computed on every render from `SP.deriveSite(project)` and the beds as they are:

| Figure | Source | L-shaped replay |
|---|---|---|
| Site area | `deriveSite().site_m2` (area of the outline now) | 40.9 m² |
| Plantable area | `deriveSite().plantable_m2` (site minus open areas and no-plant zones) | 33.9 m² |
| Bed area | sum of the active beds' `area_m2` | 14.6 m² in 3 beds |
| Last proposal | `proposals.site.plantable_m2`, shown only when the proposal is stale and differs, in its own line | "proposed for an earlier outline (40.32 m² plantable)" |

A non-rectangular site reads "40.9 m² site, within 8 × 6 m"; a rectangle still reads "8 × 6 m". While the proposal is stale the
layout summary uses the current bed and plantable figures, the option cards are marked "earlier outline", and the comparison
list of the other options is not shown (its differences were computed for another outline).

Other readers checked: the ground key (`free_m2`) already used `deriveSite`; Compose, Grow, Trace and About read areas from the
version's own evaluation or from `E.freeArea` on the current bed, and a version made for an earlier outline is already marked by
the Grow banner. No other reader of `proposals.site.*` prints a figure.

Test: spatial X15 (replay `?script=v42shape&fresh=1&stop=lshape`).

## 2.2 No Screen plants on the North bed of the trimmed outline — bug, fixed at its cause

**Seen.** `08-shape-generated-in-outline-1440x900.png`: North bed, Screen 0 / 8.2 m, 24 plants in the design.

**Chain, as traced on the replay (`stop=generated`).**

1. Boundary: the north-east corner is trimmed, so the outline has a diagonal edge from (6.5, 6) to (8, 4.5).
2. Bed: the North bed is clipped to it: `[[0,4.8],[7.7,4.8],[6.5,6],[0,6]]`, 8.52 m², 1.2 m deep along its straight edge.
3. Screen edge: `edgesFor(…, 'north-edge')` takes every bed edge on the boundary whose outward normal is within 50° of north. The
   diagonal faces north-east (45°), so it counts: two stretches, 6.5 m (straight) and 1.7 m (chamfer), 8.2 m together. That is
   reasonable: the chamfer is part of the fence line.
4. Depth: `edgeDepth()` probes the bed behind each stretch at five points and keeps the shallowest. Behind the straight stretch
   it is 1.2 m. Behind the chamfer the bed tapers to its acute corner: 0.25 m.
5. **Candidates (the bug).** `assemble()` took `Math.min` of the stretch depths as "the bed depth" for the geometry check. With
   0.25 m every Screen species failed ("needs 0.57 m … the bed has 0.25 m" for salal, 0.95 m for evergreen huckleberry), so
   `picks.screen` was empty and the edge zone was skipped with "no eligible species".
6. Generation itself (`generateBed`) already plans each stretch on its own with `rowPlan`, and placement checks every point with
   `placeable`. It never got there.
7. Validation and evaluation were correct for what they were given: no plant, cover 0 of 8.2 m.

So a 1.7 m chamfer vetoed a 6.5 m edge that takes a row. Not a legitimate refusal.

**Fix (`design/js/engine.js`, `assemble`).** The geometry check reads the depth stretch by stretch. A species passes when its
row fits at least one stretch, and the detail says how much of the edge that is ("a row fits on 6.5 m of the 8.2 m edge … 1.7 m
of the edge is only 0.25 m deep and stays without a row"). A bed with one stretch reads exactly as before. In `generateBed` a
stretch whose row plan has no row is now reported in `unresolved` with the row plan's own reason. Nothing is placed by force:
the chamfer stays unplanted and is counted as a gap.

| Replay, seed 42 | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Screen species eligible on the North bed | 0 | salal, evergreen huckleberry |
| Screen plants on the North bed | 0 | 7 (one row on the straight stretch) |
| Cover | 0 of 8.2 m | 6.91 of 8.2 m |
| Chamfer (1.7 m) | silently part of the 0 | no row; reason stated; a gap in the evaluation |
| Plant objects in the design | 24 | 31 |
| Validation errors | 0 | 0 |

The Screen status of that bed is still "not met" on continuity, because of the chamfer gap. That is the model's honest reading.

Effect on existing gardens: none. All demo and reference beds have one Screen stretch (engine test P8 compares the wording and
the picks; Demo A, the 8 × 6 m double-row example and the reference garden generate the same counts — see the suite figures).

Not changed, for a person to judge: `edgeDepth` keeps the shallowest of five probes for a stretch, so a tapering stretch is
either planted in full or not at all; the first metre of this chamfer is deep enough for salal but gets no row.

Tests: engine P5–P8; spatial X16.

## 2.3 Accent placed where validation refuses it — bug, fixed

**Reproduced.** Reference garden (6 × 4 m, part shade, ecology wanted), ground layer set to yarrow + kinnikinnick (the mix the
V4.2 first run produced), seed 42: blanketflower (*Gaillardia aristata*) becomes the accent.

**Cause.** The accent step does not place plants. It takes ground-layer positions (every fourth, nearest the open zone) and
changes their species. Those positions were checked with the ground plant's crown radius (0.15 m). The accent's radius is
0.23 m, and `placeable()` requires the distance to the bed edge to be at least half the radius. A ground position between
0.075 m and 0.115 m from the edge is valid for the ground plant and invalid for the accent, and `validate()` then reports the
generator's own plant as "too close to the bed edge".

**Fix (`generateBed`).** A position is given to the accent only if `placeable()` — the same function `validate()` calls — passes
with the accent's own radius; otherwise it stays a ground plant. The number of positions not used and the reason are recorded on
the accent group (`params.skipped`, `params.skipped_note`).

| Mix above, seed 42 | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Accent positions the old rule converts | 18 | 16 kept, 2 refused |
| Validation errors | 2 ("too close to the bed edge") | 0 |
| Reference garden with its own mix (accent yarrow, 0.15 m) | 41 plants | 41 plants, no position refused |

Neighbour spacing is unchanged: crowding is a warning in `validate()`, not an error, and the accent step never checked it.

Tests: engine P1–P4 (P2 asserts that the mix does hit the old failure, so the test cannot pass by avoiding it).

## 2.4 Light: precise shade words on the Site page, reasons beside the status

Gate logic of V4.2 unchanged. Added:

- **Site conditions** offer five light words: full sun, part shade, filtered shade, deep shade, and "shade, not sure which kind"
  (the vague word of V4.2, still conditional and never a new exclusion).
- **Kinds kept apart** on every light result (`gate.light.kind`, `gate.light.short`): `mismatch` (the plant records no such
  light: the only kind that excludes), `site-unknown`, `no-record`, `evidence-qualified` (a match whose every claim carries a
  qualifier), `site-vague`, `site-assumed`, `canopy`.
- **Compose** prints the short reason beside the status of a selected plant when its light is conditional or does not match
  ("light conditional: “shade” is not precise: filtered or deep?"); the full detail stays in the row.
- **Evidence standing** (claims, sources, how many a person checked, AI source-check counts, recorded disagreements, and the
  sentence that an AI source check is not a verification) is printed under the light row of the Decision Trace only.
- Supporting changes so the new words behave: the canopy step knows them (filtered → deep, deep → deep), and the height proxy
  treats any shade word as shade.

**Consequence of the precise words (for Kay).** They exclude, by the V4.2 rule "a precise word with no matching label fails":

| Site light | Fails (excluded) | Conditional | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| filtered shade | yarrow, kinnikinnick | Japanese maple, katsura, Pacific dogwood, red-osier dogwood, blanketflower, salal | sword fern, evergreen huckleberry |
| deep shade | Japanese maple, yarrow, kinnikinnick, katsura, Pacific dogwood, red-osier dogwood, blanketflower | — | salal, sword fern, evergreen huckleberry |

These exclusions were computable in V4.2 but unreachable, because no page offered the words. Offering them makes them reachable.
"shade, not sure which kind" still excludes nothing.

## Section 07 items

- **Snap.** Corners snap to 5 cm by default; the outline editor shows "Snap corners to 5 cm" with its state; off, a corner lands
  to the millimetre. Sketch outlines keep whole sketch units. Test: spatial X17.
- **Removing a bed.** When an outline would leave a bed under the smallest bed size (0.6 m²) or wholly outside, the review shows
  a separate warning naming the bed, its area now and the reason, Apply is disabled, and `commit()` refuses until the user ticks
  "Remove … and apply". Undo restores the bed. Test: spatial X18.
