# V4.2 light gate — qualification, not exclusion (2026-11-16)

Internal report (not for a public page). Branch `feature/platform-v4-2-ux-simplification`. Builds on
`eval/v4-1-light-gate-audit-2026-11-16.md`, which found the V4.1 gate correct as written but too certain: all ten species passed
full sun and shade in an open bed, because any one matching label passed and `shade` accepted Part sun/part shade.

**No claim, plant file, source or snapshot was changed. Nothing was marked verified. No species is excluded by the new logic that the
old logic kept.** Code: `design/js/knowledge.js` (`LIGHT`, `lightClaimQualifiers`, `lightEvidence`, `lightGate`, the canopy
branch of `gate`); one wording line in `design/js/engine.js` (`siteCheck` now prints the reason of a conditional gate).

## 1. What the gate now keeps apart

| Side | States | Read from |
|---|---|---|
| Site | unknown · assumed/inferred · stated; and, when stated, whether the word is precise | `site.conditions.light` (bed override first), the canopy inference of M55 |
| Plant evidence, per reading of the site word | supports · qualified · none · not recorded | the species' `exposure` cell: labels, claims, each claim's cultivar, context, conditions, AI source check, and the cell's recorded disagreements |
| Evidence standing (reported, never decisive) | claims, sources, human-verified count, AI check counts, recorded disagreements | the same cell; returned as `gate.light.evidence.standing` |

A claim behind a label is **clean** when it is stated for the species (no `cultivar`, and its recorded `context` does not open
with var. / subsp. / f. / cultivar), carries no condition, and the AI source check does not mark it `partially_supported` or
`conflicting_evidence`. A reading **supports** when at least one clean claim records one of its labels. It is **qualified** when the
label is recorded but no claim behind it is clean; the qualifiers are listed in the result's `detail`. One more qualifier exists for
a label that a single claim records when that claim is one side of a recorded source disagreement; no cell of the current snapshot
triggers it alone, and it is tested on an in-memory copy.

Human verification is deliberately **not** a qualifier: 47 of the 48 exposure claims are unverified, so it would turn every pass
into a conditional and the gate would carry no information. It is reported beside the result instead. "Does it fit" and "how far has
a person checked the evidence" are two statements, never one badge.

Labels are now matched exactly. Before, a substring match let "Full sun only if soil kept moist" count as "Full sun".

## 2. Decision table

| Site light | Evidence | Result | Reason given |
|---|---|---|---|
| unknown | any | `unknown` | site light unknown; the plant's labels are listed |
| any | no exposure claim | `not recorded` | — |
| stated, precise word (full-sun, part-shade; filtered-shade, deep-shade if a page offers them) | supports | `pass` | the labels recorded |
| stated, precise | qualified | `conditional` | each qualifier: cultivar or variety scope, condition, AI check partial or conflicting, lone disputed source |
| stated, precise | none | `fail` | "plant records only …" — unchanged from before |
| stated `shade` (vague: filtered or deep) | supports under **both** readings | `pass` | "fits either reading" |
| stated `shade` | at least one reading supported or qualified | `conditional` | which reading is recorded, which is not, the qualifiers, "say which shade it is" |
| stated `shade` | only Part sun/part shade recorded | `conditional` | "lighter than either" reading — never a pass through that label, never a new fail |
| stated `shade` | nothing shade-ward recorded | `fail` | unchanged from before (no species in this snapshot) |
| assumed or inferred | supports or qualified | `conditional` | the site light is not stated |
| assumed or inferred | none | `fail` | unchanged |
| under a canopy (M55: between the stated light and one step shadier) | shadier end recorded | `conditional`, never `pass` | the range, plus the qualifiers of both ends |
| under a canopy | only the open end recorded | `unknown` | unchanged |

`overall` is computed as before (fail > conditional > all pass > unknown), with one guard. In the engine's ranking `conditional`
outranks `unknown`, so a species whose light is conditional only because its evidence is thin would have been preferred to a species
with clean light evidence whenever water or soil is still unknown (a first run showed exactly that: yarrow displaced kinnikinnick as
the ground layer, 92 plants instead of 41). The guard: a light result that is conditional on evidence alone (site stated, no canopy;
`gate.light.evidence_only`) leaves `overall` at `unknown` while water or soil is unknown, as it was before V4.2. When water and soil
are known, such a species is `conditional` and ranks after a passing one. Qualified evidence can therefore lower a species' rank and
never raise it (test LG17). The demonstration gardens state no light, so their arrangements, counts and evaluations do not change.

Seen on the way, not changed (engine, outside this work): with that displaced mix the generator placed one accent plant
(*Gaillardia aristata*, bed 3) that its own validation rejects as "too close to the bed edge". The mix no longer occurs, but accent
placement can evidently produce a position that `validate` refuses.

## 4. Tests

`design/tests.html` (run by `tools/design_tests.py`): A3 rewritten on a real pass; **A4 fixed** (it accepted `fail || pass` and
could not fail; it now requires a fail on a precise word with no matching label). New section LG1–LG17, one test per row of the
table on the snapshot's own claims: unknown site; clean pass; AI-partial → conditional; variety scope + condition + AI conflict →
conditional; a cultivar claim never carries a pass; both shade readings → pass; one reading → conditional; lighter label only →
conditional; assumed site; **LG10 no new exclusion** (the old rule is recomputed in the test and compared cell by cell); standing
separate from the result; the lone-disputed-source rule on a copy; canopy; the precise words; the engine's assemblage; no promotion by qualified evidence.
`tools/platform_tests.py` G1: the derived-version line of the Trace page.

## 5. Where the result is read

The gate's `result` and `detail` are printed as they are by Compose (eligibility rows, "site gate …"), the Decision Trace (the
light / water / soil table), About · How it works (through `T.decisions`) and the schedule export. They need no change to show the
new status and reason. The Plant Profile does not show a gate (it has no site).

Wording that would be better with a small edit in files this change does not own:
- `design/js/app.js` `COND.light` offers full-sun / part-shade / shade. Offering "filtered shade" and "deep shade" (already read
  by the gate) would let a user answer the gate's question "say which shade it is".
- Compose's summary chip prints only `site gate conditional`; the reason is in the row's detail and tooltip.
- Nothing yet prints `evidence.standing` (verified n of m, AI check counts) beside a light result; the data is on the gate object.

## 6. Not applied — decisions for a person

1. Whether `shade` should stop accepting a plant that records only Part sun/part shade (today: conditional). That would be a new
   exclusion of up to 2 species (yarrow, kinnikinnick) and is a horticultural judgement.
2. Whether a qualified match should ever exclude (e.g. sword fern in full sun, whose only full-sun claims are for var. imbricans,
   conditioned on summer water, or marked conflicting). Today: conditional.
3. Whether AI "partially supported" should keep counting as a qualifier. It is an AI reading of the source, not a human judgement;
   by itself it decides 2 of the 11 conditional cells (yarrow and blanketflower in part shade); in the other nine another
   reason also applies. The alternative is to report it only as standing.
4. Whether the canopy step should distinguish part shade from shade (it cannot today: the shadier end of part shade is the vague
   word).
5. Whether the same qualification should be applied to water and soil. They are unchanged in V4.2.
6. Whether the Site page should offer the precise words filtered shade / deep shade.

## 7. Derived versions on the Trace page (§9.2)

generation reads "Reading version N, a partial regeneration / typed edit / manual edit of version M", then the design's name, a
change summary counted on the plant objects (added, removed, moved, changed species, n → m plants) and the version's own label; it
is never called generated, and the line also shows on the demonstration garden (Demo A's current version is a partial regeneration
of version 1, 41 → 38 plants). A preview says "Reading a preview"; a generated version "Reading generated version N". Version ids,

never sit in the same sentence. Styles: one line in `platform/css/platform.css` (`.kn-case-read`, `.kn-case-ids`).
