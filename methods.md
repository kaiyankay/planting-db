# Methods log — parameters and conventions decided while building the db

Every number or rule that is not a survey result and not a schema decision goes here: what it is, where it applies, why, who decided, when. Read with `HANDOFF.md` §3 (decisions) and `schema.json` rules R1–R10. When a parameter changes, add a new entry and mark the old one superseded; do not edit history.

Format: **M<n> · name** · value · applies to · rationale · decided by · date.

---

**M1 · cov_trees confidence baselines** · presence 0.95 · counts / local-area distribution 0.90 · cultivar label frequencies 0.85 · DBH distribution 0.80 · height-class distribution 0.75 · age cohort (trees with `date_planted`) 0.75 · established proxy (M2) 0.65 · applies to every claim with `source_id: cov_trees` · rationale: open municipal inventory is reliable on *presence* and *counts*; height is a banded class (M4) so lower; a proxy must rank below a direct measurement · decided by Kay (review of T1, 2026-10-29) with figures proposed by the coding agent · 2026-10-29.

**M2 · "established" proxy for trees without a planting date** · DBH ≥ 20 cm · applies to `local_mature_size` claims on the subset of trees that have no `date_planted`; the threshold is written into each claim's `value.threshold_dbh_cm` and `context`, and recorded in `sources.json → cov_trees.methods` · rationale: the 2026-10-26 snapshot carries no planting dates, so age cannot be derived; 20 cm DBH is a reasonable "past establishment" mark for a small tree like *Acer palmatum*. The wording is always *established (DBH ≥ 20 cm, proxy)*, never "mature". Trees **with** a planting date go to age-cohort claims (`time_to_intended_size`, 0.75) and are never mixed with the proxy subset · decided by Kay · 2026-10-29.

**M2a · minimum sample for an established-proxy claim** · n ≥ 20 trees in the subset; below that no proxy claim is emitted and the group's height-class claim says so in its `note` · applies to every group in M3 (on the 2026-10-26 snapshot this drops the proxy claim for 'Bloodgood' (3 trees) and 'Koto No Ito' (5)) · rationale: a median of three trees is an anecdote, not a distribution · proposed by the coding agent, **confirmed by Kay (D3, 2026-10-29)**.

**M3 · cultivar claim groups** · a cultivar label with n ≥ 50 records in the CoV inventory gets its own group of size claims, `context` = `cultivar:'Name'; CoV public trees, snapshot …`; labels below 50 stay inside the species-level claims and are listed by name and count in those claims' `note`; species-level claims are always kept, so one field shows "species overall" and "cultivar X" side by side; unlabelled trees (`cultivar_name` = NONE) form their own class `cultivar:(unrecorded)` and are never merged into any cultivar · applies to `local_mature_size` and, when dates exist, `time_to_intended_size` from `cov_trees` · rationale: *Acer palmatum* is in the prototype because of cultivar ambiguity; 407 Osakazuki records are a real sample; the (unrecorded) class is not the straight species · decided by Kay · 2026-10-29. Current groups (snapshot 2026-10-26): Osakazuki 407, Bloodgood 88, Koto No Ito 65, (unrecorded) 1,103.

**M4 · height_m is a class, not a measurement** · CoV `height_m` values cluster at 4.6 / 7.6 / 10.7 / 13.7 / 16.8 m (15 / 25 / 35 / 45 / 55 ft midpoints), ~97 % of *Acer palmatum* records · applies to every CoV height claim: `value.measure = "height class midpoint"`, text says "height class", confidence per M1 · rationale: quoting 7.6 m as a measured median would overstate precision · observed and decided by the coding agent, confirmed by Kay · 2026-10-29.

**M8 · licence tiers and the T5 extraction protocol** · tier A open data (import values, e.g. cov_trees); tier B cite facts, never copy text (rhs, mobot, gpp, osu, iscbc, eflora, pojar); tier B-restrictive: terms forbid storing material, kept only for an absence-of-listing fact, Kay decides (aspca); tier C never import (kpu). **M28 · graph build and diagnosis rules (`tools/build_graph.py` → `brain/data/graph.json`, `diagnosis.json`; `brain/js/diagnosis.js` is the same rules in the browser)** · nodes: concepts, plants, cultivars (from `cultivar`), sources and anonymous practitioners; edges: one per claim per concept (`spanned: true` for classes a range spans), entity facts (plant type, origin status) as `entity` edges, relations as plant–plant edges; fields are not nodes. Diagnosis: **hub** = concept linked to ≥ 2 plants by live sourced edges; **variation** = same plant + facet, two claims with different stated conditions and non-overlapping values (cultivar must match); **disagreement** = same plant + facet, same or no stated conditions, different sources, non-overlapping values — for size facets both the numeric intervals must be disjoint *and* no KPU class shared (2.44 m and 2.4 m are both '2 - 3m'), for other ordinal facets no shared concept, open lists never; **gaps** = (a) plant without a live claim on a gate facet (any hardiness scale counts), (b) plant–concept link on one source, (c) two plants sharing ≥ 5 concepts with no live relation; **single voice** = concept whose links come from one source type. Replaces the v2 `values differ` heuristic (M24) entirely. Group concepts inherit their children's links in the brain (Shade = filtered + deep shade) · to confirm · 2026-10-31.

**M29 · growth-form classification layer (`ontology.json` dimension `classification`, facet `growth_form`; `tools/build_graph.py` classification edges; brain v4.1)** · six organising nodes — Tree · Shrub · Graminoid · Herbaceous perennial · Fern · Vine or climber — sit above the knowledge dimensions; they are not a KPU facet and the KPU plant type stays a trait. A plant's form is *read from its sources*, never inferred from "Broadleaf evergreen" (a leaf habit): votes come from the KPU type where it names a form (Tree - deciduous, Shrub - deciduous, Poales, Bamboo, Fern, Vine, Conifer → tree), from form words (tree, shrub, grass/sedge/bamboo, fern, vine/climber, herbaceous perennial) in the text of claims on habit, form, height, spread, growth rate, landscape use, behaviour, long-term performance, Vancouver context and texture, and from the survey respondent's typed role. A mention that occurs only in `landscape_use` or `vancouver_context` is *weak* (RHS "mixed shrub border" says what a plant is planted with, not what it is). Status **supported** needs at least one strong non-survey source and at least two sources in all; otherwise **to confirm**. Today: *Acer palmatum* → tree (supported: KPU type, GPP growth-rate text, survey; the City of Vancouver tree inventory counted weak), *Gaultheria shallon* → shrub (supported: OSU habit, E-Flora height text, survey; OSU also says "fern-like", recorded as another mention), *Vaccinium ovatum* → shrub (**to confirm**: RHS use mention + survey only). Classification edges carry the evidence claim ids and are excluded from the gate-facet gap rule. The brain's home reads the system in four layers (classification → knowledge dimensions → plants → evidence) and the Diagnosis is an analysis view; the walk trail names each step's role (01 Classification · Shrub → 02 Plant trait · Evergreen → …) · to confirm · 2026-10-31.

**M30 · Neural Brain v5 — visual encodings and activation rules (`brain/js/app5.js`)** · the store is drawn as one network: every ontology concept is a neuron (in use = bright, sized by linked plants; dormant = small, dim, further out), plants and growth forms are the central somas, sources are ringed glows in the Evidence cluster; each claim is a synapse on the plant → concept fibre (white verified · cluster colour unverified · magenta candidate); fibres from one plant into one cluster share a stem (the v4 bundling, in 3D). Eight clusters have fixed anchors (Classification at the centre, seven on a sphere); every node has a home point near its anchor and a weak force relaxes it; a 3,500-particle field (neuropil) around clusters and paths is decoration, not data. Activation is a breadth-first walk over the real links, one hop per ≈ 0.5 s with per-fibre jitter, with rules per seed: concept → children → plants → growth form + sources; plant → all its links → parents; growth form → plants → shared concepts + sources; source → plants → its own claims; cluster → its in-use neurons → plants; question → named concepts → gated plants (R7) → the retriever's ranked claims (R5) → their sources, L0 withheld (R2), candidates recalled never cited. Growth: a candidate's fibres extend from the plant over 1.4 s, then fire. Candidates stay in `localStorage` (`brain.candidates.v5`); nothing is written to the db · to confirm · 2026-10-31.

## M55 — Design logic, first repair round: site conditions, bed functions, unsupported functions (2026-11-14, *to confirm*)

Repairs to the existing design engine after the implementation audit; no new feature, no workflow or interface redesign. (M54 is the survey increment on branch `survey/increment-2026-11-14`.) **Soil.** The plant facet `soil` mixes three dimensions (texture · pH · drainage); the site has two conditions (`soil_type`, `soil_drainage`). Each site value is judged only inside its own dimension: the plant records the wanted label → pass (conditional when the site value is assumed); the plant records another label of that dimension *and* some species in the database records the wanted one → fail; otherwise → **unknown**. So clay, loam and poorly drained — which no species records — read unknown for every plant instead of failing all ten, and "well-drained" is read from `soil_drainage`, where the interpreter stores it. The two results are reported separately and one unassessed part keeps the soil gate at unknown. **Light under a canopy.** A bed with at least half of its area (**heuristic** `HEURISTICS.canopy_share_min` = 0.5, 0.2 m sample grid — a threshold chosen by the implementer, not from a source, named in the assemblage note it produces) under existing tree crowns, and no light stated for the bed itself, is checked against a *range*: the site's stated light to one step shadier. A species that records the shadier case is conditional, one that records only the open case is unknown, never pass; the light value is not rewritten to shade, and an unknown site light stays unknown. **Directions.** The screened edge is read from the clause that asks for privacy, else from the text without the tree / open-area evidence; only a compass side (or back / front, with the plan-up assumption listed) sets an edge. Left / right / middle — or no side at all ("privacy along the fence") — set none: the function is recorded with `where: edge-unset`, **awaiting the user's choice**. Nothing is decided for the user: the proposal draws no screen band on any edge, the engine plants no row, the Shape page lists the pending choice among its constraints and assumptions, the Compose page shows "edge not chosen" with an edge selector, and every version lists it under unresolved. `boundary` (every outer edge) now exists only as an explicit user choice. A tree's or an open area's direction is never taken as the screened edge, even inside the same sentence. Demo A and the test fixtures that need a screen now say "privacy along the north fence" (the earlier wording got its north band only through that leak). An unknown direction on a function gives no edge (it fell back to north). **Functions control the planting.** `FUNCTION_SUPPORT` declares what the engine can arrange: Screen (along_edge, stagger) and Ground Cover (fill_drift, repeat_accent). A bed is planted only for the functions it carries (its own list, or the site-wide list when it has none): no ground layer without Ground Cover, no row without Screen, nothing at all without a function. Scrim, Ceiling / Canopy and Barrier are **recorded only**: no pick, no zone, no plant, a line in `unresolved`, and a label in the interface; no arrangement rule was invented for them. A screen row that cannot fit a bed at the claimed spread is now reported instead of silently skipped. **Site-wide ↔ bed.** A proposal gives every bed the site-wide functions that apply to bed interiors; adding or removing a site-wide function on the Compose page is carried to the beds it concerns (a bed "lies along" an edge when at least half of its longest side runs on it — **heuristic** `HEURISTICS.edge_share_min` = 0.5, likewise not from a source and named in the Compose message when it leaves a bed unchanged; a function set by hand on one bed is never removed; a bed with no list of its own keeps following the site-wide list) and the design is marked stale. **Recorded-only conditions.** Wind and irrigation, and soil values the database cannot express, say so beside their control (`Knowledge.conditionUse`). Both 50 % thresholds stay as labelled heuristics until reviewed. Tests: `design/js/tests.js` R1–R29, `tools/design_tests.py` F1–F8 (`?script=fn`).

## M56 — Knowledge-based planting arrangement: selection classes, row and area arithmetic, union-based evaluation (2026-11-14, *to confirm*)

Round 2, engine only (`knowledge.js`, `engine.js`); no page structure, plant or graph change. **Selection.** For Screen and Ground Cover every species is classed *meets · uncertain · fails* from separate checks — function-link evidence (a candidate link or a link with a conflict is uncertain at best), site gates, and for Screen the target height (read on all claimed height ranges: none reaches → fails; the planned range starts at or above and none stays below → meets; else uncertain), leaf retention when year-round is required, tree form (no tree-placement rule) and the bed depth behind the edge. *Fails* is never planted; a second row species only joins when it is in the same class as the first. The Screen requirement lives on the function: the target height defaults to 1.8 m as a **labelled heuristic** when not stated, a year-round requirement is never assumed; both can be said in words. **Arrangement.** `rowPlan` computes, per screened edge, whether one row and a staggered second row fit the bed depth, the spacing (0.75 × claimed mature spread; the mean of two spreads when two species alternate) and the positions; the ground layer reports its plantable area (bed − no-plant zones − trunk keep-out − screen crowns) and a lattice estimate beside the placed count. A latent fault was fixed: an alternating row was spaced by its first species only. **Evaluation.** Screen: crowns are projected on the edge and the union of the intervals is the covered length (overlaps once), with located gaps; the same union for plants that certainly / possibly reach the target height and for evergreen-only species; status *not met · uncertain · met on claimed sizes*, never "verified" (`verified_performance: false`). Ground Cover: share of the plantable area under ground-layer crowns, labelled a geometric estimate. `evaluation.findings` lists every missing-data, conflict, uncertain and unmet item with its reason. **Sources.** `ARRANGEMENT_RULES` names each parameter as claim, geometry or heuristic; the database holds no spacing claim, so both spacing factors, the tolerances and the default target height are heuristics. Rules and results: `reports/arrangement-algorithm-2026-11-14.md`. Tests: `design/js/tests.js` V1–V24, `tools/design_tests.py` G1–G8 (`?script=r2`, Demo A).
