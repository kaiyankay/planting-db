## What changed

| | Before (V1) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Destinations | Design · Plants · Explore · Contribute (+ How it works) | **Design · Plants · Knowledge**; inside Knowledge: Overview · Explore · Research · Method |
| Knowledge entry | the Explorer's five panes at once | an overview that follows one real decision from a source to an evaluated bed, in five steps |

## The worked example is real

The overview's case is `designs/demo-a.json`, a project the Studio itself saved: a 6 × 4 m garden, "privacy along the north
fence". The page loads it, imports the Studio's engine, re-checks all ten species for the North bed and re-measures the row.
What it shows on 2026-11-14:

- **Source statement**: USDA PLANTS, "Hedge Tolerance: High".
- **Structured claim**: `gaultheria-shallon:landscape_use:usdaplants01`, landscape use = sheared hedge, confidence 0.6.
- **Human verification**: unverified.
- **AI source assessment**: partially supported — the page rates a tolerance, it does not recommend the use.
- **Rule-derived relationship**: salal → Screen, derived by KG-R1; height, density and evergreen requirements met by claims.
- **Site information**: outline stated; light, water and soil unknown; no target height stated.
- **Engine output**: salal is the only species that passes (uncertain); 10 positions, 7 placed, 3 dropped under the tree.
- **Evaluation**: **Screen: not met** — 1.61 m of 6 m uncovered, target height uncertain. Recomputed on the page: same result.

Nothing of this is written in the page code (`tools/platform_tests.py` K1–K6 compare the page with the snapshot and with the
recorded design). When the visitor has a design of their own with a Screen bed, the same trace runs on it; a design that was
never generated is traced on the engine's dry run, as Compose previews it.

## Limits

- One function is traced into a design: Screen. Ground Cover is arranged by the engine too but has no trace page; Canopy,
  Scrim and Barrier have no arrangement rule at all.
- The reference case is one garden. Its verdict (not met) is what the engine computes; another site would give another story.
- The "source statement" row shows the excerpt the AI source check recorded, at most a sentence or two. For a survey
  response there is deliberately none.
- Ten species; 38 of 501 claims verified by a person. The AI source assessment was read by an agent, not by the API pipeline.
- The illustrations are AI-generated and unreviewed botanically. They are labelled so and never used as evidence.
- Research shows no survey findings, only what the released snapshot holds and how the structure changed.
- The public build reads descriptions with offline rules. No language model is connected.
- Explorer and Studio are desktop workspaces; the reading pages (Overview, Research, Method, Library) adapt to a phone.
