## The idea

The plan is drawn the way a landscape drawing is, and its conventions are the interface:

- **line style says where a fact comes from** — solid: stated or confirmed · dashed: suggested or estimated · dotted: assumed. The same three lines appear on the site model, in its legend and on the evidence groups.
- **hatching says what a bed is for** — diagonal hatch along the edge: Screen · stipple: Ground Cover · an empty dashed box: a function that is recorded only (Scrim, Canopy, Barrier). The same marks appear on the function plan, its legend, the bed tabs and the bed rows of Page 2.

No metric was added: everything shown comes from `E.assemble`, `E.rowPlan`, `E.freeArea`, `E.edgesFor` and `E.bedConditions`. Spacing is still labelled a heuristic wherever it is shown.

## Interaction reference

The vertex / mid-edge / delete model follows the spatial-editing pattern of the SVG Overlay Editor (StehlikOndrej1/svg-overlay-editor, MIT; React — pattern only, no code taken): drag a corner, drag a mid-edge handle to add a corner, right-click or double-click a corner to remove it, Esc leaves the edit.
