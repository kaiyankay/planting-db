# Platform V3 — a universal Decision Trace (2026-11-14)

Branch `feature/platform-integration` · methods M67 · preview https://kaiyankay.github.io/planting-db/platform-preview/ ·
screenshots `brain/screenshots/platform-v3/`. The database, the knowledge graph, the planting algorithm, the Imagine hero and
the Shape / Compose editing framework are unchanged.

## How knowledge, rules and design output connect

```
source page ──▶ claim ──▶ function link ──▶ selection for a bed ──▶ arrangement ──▶ evaluation
 (a source)    (record,    (rule KG-R1 or     (engine: site gates,     (engine: row or    (engine: met ·
               person,      KG-R4 over the     function evidence,       pattern, spacing   uncertain · not met,
               AI check)    claims)            size proxy, geometry)    heuristics, seed)  or an estimate)
```

Every arrow is something the page can open: the excerpt and where it sits on the source page, the claim with its id, who
verified it, what the AI source assessment found, the rule that drew the link, the checks the engine ran for the bed with the
claim each one read, the plant objects and the numbers that placed them, and the engine's own measurement of the result.

One module (`platform/js/trace.js`) reads the engine's records and nothing else. The Knowledge overview shows the full trace;
the Studio shows the same trace in five lines ("Why this plant here?"). There is no second copy of a planting rule.

## What a species can be, in a bed

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| planted | in the mix, placed by the arrangement |
| selected, not placed | in the mix; the arrangement gave it no position |
| eligible, not selected | passed the checks; the mix is other species |
| excluded by a check | a check failed, with a claim or a measurement against it |
| evidence unknown | a claim the function needs is missing; not a judgement of the plant |
| planted for another function | already the Screen row of this bed |
| recorded only | the function has no arrangement rule |
| not asked of this bed | the bed was not given the function |

## Three journeys (replayed by `design/?script=trace`, checked by `tools/platform_tests.py` J1–J7)

All three start from the Studio's own demonstration sentence, "a 6 × 4 m backyard with a tree in the northwest corner … privacy
along the north edge and an open centre", and are driven through the page's own controls.

**A — Screen.** Generate → Grow → select a planted *Gaultheria shallon* of the north row → *Why this plant here?* The inspector
answers in five lines: the bed was asked to be a Screen along its north edge (read from "privacy" by offline rules; light,
water and soil not stated); the link to Screen rests on one USDA PLANTS claim, unverified, which the AI source assessment
found partially supported; the species is *planted* (evidence meets, site uncertain, height uncertain, row fits); ten
positions at 0.59 m, seven placed, three dropped; **Screen: not met**, 4.27 of 6 m covered. *Open the full trace* shows the
same design version, the same plant object ringed on the plan, the source excerpt, the claim, the rule and the engine's
checks, re-measured with the same result. *Back to Grow* restores the stage, the selected plant and the open explanation.

**B — Ground Cover.** Compose → East bed → the three arrangement options are three different sets of plant objects (19, 26
and 16 plants) computed by the engine. Choose *Clustered patches*, generate: 16 of 18 positions placed, estimate 67 %. Select
a ground plant → the explanation names the pattern, its patches and the estimate. Back in Compose choose *Rhythmic rows*,
generate again: a new version, 26 of 26 placed, estimate 81 %. The same trace link now describes the new version, pattern,
count and estimate; the earlier ones are gone from the page. Both generated versions are plant for plant the previews.

**C — Excluded species.** With *Acer palmatum*, *Polystichum munitum* and *Gaillardia aristata* considered from the Plant
Library, the East bed (Ground Cover) shows three rows with their own outcomes: *Acer palmatum* is **excluded by a check**
(no source records it as ground cover, and its recorded height bands are outside what Ground Cover requires); *Polystichum
munitum* and *Gaillardia aristata* are **eligible, not selected** (they pass, the mix is two other species; Gaillardia's
link is only inferred from traits) and can be added. *Why not here?* opens the explanation, and the full trace gives each
species the same outcome for the same bed. In the recorded demonstration garden the same bed also shows a **selected, not
placed** case: *Gaultheria shallon* is in the engine's mix and the drift pattern gave every position to the other species.
No species of this snapshot is *evidence unknown* for Screen or Ground Cover: every missing link there is contradicted by a
recorded trait (the three "evidence missing" links belong to the functions that are recorded only).

## What the independent critique changed

A separate review of the rendered pages at 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800 (Impeccable method) found 25 problems; the ones fixed in
this round: the decision is now a five-node chain in the first viewport; the step bar marks the step being read; the claim
id is carried through steps 3–5; "unknown" names which kind of missing link it is; "supported" is kept for the AI source
assessment; rows that record an absence no longer carry a maker tag; the pair comparison moved out of the five steps;
record ids break only after a colon and are dropped from sentences; Method's rule table, the Library's filter row and the
Explorer's matrix, table, graph key, focus mark and Back behaviour at 1280 px. Left for a later round: Compose's floating
evidence panel still covers part of the plan, and its inspector repeats the Screen verdict in several places.

## Limits that affect a design decision

- **Two functions are arranged**: Screen and Ground Cover. Canopy, Scrim and Barrier are recorded only; the trace stops after
  the knowledge steps for them and shows no planting.
- **Ground Cover has no pass mark.** The engine reports the share of plantable ground under claimed mature crowns and sets no
  threshold; the trace shows "an estimate" and never "met".
- **"Selected, not placed" has no per-species reason in the engine.** The trace says which species took the positions and
  which pattern ran; it cannot say more, because the engine does not record more.
- **Ten species, mostly unverified claims.** A bed often has one species that passes; outcomes say more about the size of the
  database than about the plants.
- **Site conditions are usually unknown** in a new design, so site checks read "uncertain" and nothing passes outright.
- **Estimates only.** Sizes are claimed mature ranges; no result is a measured or verified performance.
- **Offline rules** read the description in this public build. No language model is connected.
- The Studio and the Explorer are desktop workspaces (checked at 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800).
