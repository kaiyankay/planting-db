## 3. Data architecture

**Canonical (unchanged):** `plants/*.json` (claims), `relations.json`, `sources.json`, `schema.json` 0.4.3, `ontology.json` 0.2.0 → `brain/data/kg.json` (build_kg) → `brain/data/xyz.json` (build_xyz, M48). **New derived file:** `brain/data/explorer.json` (build_explorer, M49; 2.3 MB), rebuilt by `tools/pipeline.py`; updating a plant file never means editing frontend data (Test I: the interface names no id).

Logical collections (a GIS-style table is a *view*; see `brain/explorer/README.md` for the field lists): `species` 10 · `facets` 27 · `attributes` 230 cells, every distinct value kept (40 (species, facet) cells not recorded are absent and shown as *not recorded*; 27 cells carry a kg.json disagreement) · `functions` 5 with 18 literature requirements · `function_links` **exactly 50** · `concept_links` 361 · `relations` 41 (19 live, 22 retired as history) · `pairs` 45 · `claims` 501 · `sources` 17 · `evidence_checks` 534 · `rules` · `vocab`.

**Stable identifiers and evidence paths:** species `plant:<slug>` = `plants/<slug>.json`; functions `z:*` = the xyz functions standing for ontology concepts (`spatial:screen`, `spatial:ceiling` + `layer:canopy`, `role:ground_cover` + `layer:ground` …); link cells `link:<plant>><function>` name their kg design edges (`underlying`) and xyz line; attribute cells `attr:<plant>:<facet>` name their xyz lines and claims; concept links keep the xyz ids; relations keep the `relations.json` ids and their kg / xyz edge; claims keep their ids and `status` exactly as in kg.json (Test D3).

**Three separate statements on every record:** `status` documented · derived · candidate · unknown (kg vocabulary) — `verification` verified · unverified · none · entity · literature — `evidence_check` (AI assessment from `review/ai/assessments`: supported 359 · partially supported 99 · conflicting 5 of the cited claims; the 38 verified claims were not queued → *not assessed*). Matrix cells: derived 12 (a source records a use, KG-R1) · candidate 9 (every required trait met, KG-R4; never verified) · unknown 29 (26 *contradicted by traits*, 3 *evidence missing*) · 4 conflicts (a documented use against a trait claim: *Cornus sericea* and *Vaccinium ovatum* → Screen / Barrier) · verification: 3 verified (survey ground-cover uses) · 9 unverified · 38 none.

## 6. Unresolved gaps (for Kay)

- **Z evidence gaps are claim gaps, not interface gaps** (as on 2026-11-03): Scrim 1 linked / 9 unknown (no *open / twiggy / arching* habit claim at height except *Cercidiphyllum*, candidate); Ceiling 3 / 7; Screen 5 / 5; Barrier 6 / 4; Ground Cover 6 / 4. 26 of the 29 unknown cells are *contradicted by traits* (a height or habit claim outside the requirement — shown with the claim), 3 are *evidence missing* (*Acer palmatum* → Scrim / Barrier, *Arctostaphylos* → Barrier: no habit claim).
- **Conflicts kept visible, not resolved:** *Cornus sericea* and *Vaccinium ovatum* → Screen / Barrier (MOBOT / GPP / survey list hedging or screening; the habit claims are not *dense*).
- **Candidate links are inferences** (9 cells, e.g. *Cercidiphyllum* → Screen / Scrim / Barrier, *Polystichum* and *Gaillardia* → Barrier, *Acer* and *Cornus nuttallii* → Ceiling): no source states the use; they stay candidate and are never verified.
- **Verification:** 38 of the 501 cited claims are verified (City of Vancouver open data, survey); only 3 matrix cells rest on a verified use claim. Human review decisions: none in this projection (the Workbench keeps them privately; 0 decisions so far). The AI checks (534) are shown as assessments beside verification, never instead of it.
- **Attribute disagreements:** 27 cells carry a recorded source disagreement (height classes above all: *Acer palmatum* City of Vancouver vs GPP vs RHS; salal OSU sun vs shade); 40 cells are not recorded (e.g. *ecology* for five plants — a gate facet).
- **Dangling reference in `relations.json`:** `rel:gaultheria-shallon:vaccinium-ovatum:m2` names `gaultheria-shallon:behaviour_with_others:usda01`, which does not exist in the plant file; the projection lists it under `missing_claims` and the record keeps its other two supporting claims. To fix in the canonical file, not here.
- **Species-to-species knowledge is thin:** 19 live records, 15 of them survey co-listings (weak evidence by design); the only mechanism records are model placeholders (*competes*, *layered_under*) still to verify against Pojar / a practitioner. The complementarity section therefore offers *hypotheses* only.
- **Decisions to confirm (M49):** the 'use / traits / contradicted / no evidence' matrix words; the attribute-table column set (every facet the claims reach; numeric classes shown as classes, numbers in the inspector); the four A/B sections; the "different layer" reading of complementarity (overhead · above eye · knee and above · ground); the facet → group table and the Z pairings inherited from M48.
- **Not done, by the brief:** no new plants, no AI re-verification, no workbench change, no new Z concepts, no relation written, no planting generator, no site / value / layout module.

## 7. Next-phase readiness

The projection is already the interface the future modules need: **Site** can gate on `attributes` (every value with conditions, place and verification, `facets[].gate` from survey c1) without reading the claim files; **Values** can weigh `function_links` and `pairs` as evidence-carrying candidates (status, verification and AI check travel with them, so an economic or ecological weighting can say what it rests on); **Layout** can start from the complementarity combinations (distinct layers) and the typed `relations` (competes, layered_under) once they are verified. Adding a plant is unchanged: `plants/<id>.json` → `tools/pipeline.py`; it appears in all four views. What the next phase needs from the data, not the code: habit and density claims (Scrim, Screen, Barrier), verified practitioner relations, and a tier-B toxicity source.
