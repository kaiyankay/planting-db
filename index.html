Planting Intelligence

Knowledge-driven planting design: describe a site in your own words, shape it into planting beds, say what the planting should do, and generate an arrangement from a sourced, claim-level plant knowledge base (UBC MLA · ARCH 540).

Current version: platform-v4-3-preview/

  • Design: Imagine → Shape → Compose → Grow
  • Plants: the plant library
  • Knowledge: the evidence network, table and decision trace
  • About: project, how it works, method

Source code, data and reports: github.com/kaiyankay/planting-db. Practitioner survey: kaiyankay.github.io/planting-survey.
